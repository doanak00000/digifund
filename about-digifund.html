About Digifund – Vietnam Semiconductor Materials Supplier

Digifund Joint Stock Company (digifund.vn) supplies silicon wafers, substrates, semiconductor materials and equipment from Ho Chi Minh City, serving the semiconductor and renewable energy industries since 2023.

1. Who we are

Digifund is a trusted supplier of advanced materials and technical solutions for the semiconductor and renewable energy industries. Founded in 2023, we focus on connecting two key pillars of the future: semiconductors and clean energy.

We specialise in silicon wafers, substrates and essential high-quality materials for semiconductor research and production, supporting both lab-scale innovation and industrial manufacturing. Beyond materials, Digifund also supplies cleanroom and laboratory equipment, high-purity chemicals and consumables for high-tech industries.

2. Our 5 product groups

  1. Silicon Wafer & Substrate — Si (CZ/FZ), SiC, SOI, SiN, Sapphire, AlN, GaAs, GaN, InP, ITO/Glass, carrier wafers.
  2. Semiconductor Fabrication Materials — photoresist, developer, HMDS, stripper, ARC, CMP slurry & pads, sputtering targets, evaporation materials, wet-process chemicals.
  3. Advanced Packaging Materials — temporary bonding adhesives, debonding chemicals, carrier wafers, UV dicing / back grinding tape, dicing blades, Cu/Ni/Au plating chemicals, underfill, silver epoxy, die attach film, conductive adhesives, mold compound.
  4. Cleanroom & Hi-Tech Equipment — fume hoods, wet benches, glassware, cleanroom wear, spin coaters, mask aligners, direct laser writers, CVD, RIE, sputtering, microscopes, profilometers, vacuum systems, gas supply.
  5. Renewable Energy Materials & Equipment — photo-, thermo- and electro-catalytic reactors, fuel cell test stations, battery research equipment, nanomaterials.

3. Our 3 core services

  • Semiconductor materials & equipment supply — materials for the entire chipmaking process, with a plentiful, reliable supply.
  • Technical & technology consulting — a team of seasoned experts and 24/7 technical support.
  • Custom fabrication services — an EU-standard lab for chip fabrication and highly skilled, specialised engineers.

5. Collaboration & research

Digifund works with the world's leading semiconductor research institutes and universities: CNRS, IEMN (Institut d'Électronique, de Microélectronique et de Nanotechnologie), imec, Université de Lille, Gachon University, UCCS (Unité de Catalyse et de Chimie du Solide) and Griffith University.

Digifund materials and equipment are used in photocatalysis and semiconductor fabrication research published in leading international journals, including:

  • Nature Energy (2026) — Exploiting the insulator–metal transition of VO2 in photocatalytic methane conversion.
  • Applied Catalysis B: Environment and Energy (2025) — Electricity-driven selectivity in the photocatalytic oxidation of methane to carbon monoxide with liquid gallium-semiconductor composite.
  • Nature Communications (2024) — Photocatalytic dihydroxylation of light olefins to glycols by water.
  • ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces (2024) — Boosting gas-phase TiO2 photocatalysis with weak electric field strengths of volt/centimeter.

At IEMN (France), p-Si wafers serve as substrates for p-n junctions and n-Si wafers as substrates for microelectronics.
