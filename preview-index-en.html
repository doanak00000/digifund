About Digifund

Founded 2023 · Ho Chi Minh City

Digifund is a trusted supplier of advanced materials and technical solutions for the semiconductor and renewable energy industries. Founded in 2023, we focus on connecting two key pillars of the future: semiconductors and clean energy.

We specialise in silicon wafers, substrates and essential high-quality materials for semiconductor research and production, supporting both lab-scale innovation and industrial manufacturing. Beyond materials, Digifund also supplies cleanroom and laboratory equipment, high-purity chemicals and consumables for high-tech industries.

  • ISO-standard quality assurance.
  • Full technical support on every order.
  • Industry-leading wafer specifications and purity standards.

What is Digifund?

Digifund is a silicon wafer and semiconductor materials supplier in Vietnam. Digifund supplies silicon wafers (Si, SiC, GaAs, Sapphire, SOI), substrates, sputtering targets, chemicals and cleanroom equipment for the semiconductor industry, research and IC manufacturing.

Where can I buy silicon wafers in Vietnam?

You can buy silicon wafers in Vietnam through Digifund. Digifund supplies Si, SiC, GaAs, Sapphire and SOI wafers and substrates with quotes on request. Contact +84 979 324 567 or semiconductor@digifund.vn for advice.

Which wafers does Digifund supply?

Digifund supplies Si (silicon), SiC (silicon carbide), GaAs (gallium arsenide), Sapphire and SOI wafers, plus many other semiconductor substrates. See also: Types of silicon wafer.

Besides silicon wafers, what else does Digifund supply?

Digifund supplies five product groups: (1) silicon wafers and substrates; (2) semiconductor fabrication materials: photoresists, CMP materials, sputtering targets and wet-process chemicals; (3) advanced packaging materials: temporary bonding, carrier wafers, dicing consumables, plating chemistry, underfill, die attach film; (4) cleanroom and hi-tech equipment: fume hoods, wet benches, spin coaters, mask aligners, CVD, RIE and sputtering systems, and metrology; (5) renewable energy materials and equipment.
