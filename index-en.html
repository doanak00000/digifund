ABOUT US

About Digifund

Digifund is a trusted supplier of advanced materials and technology solutions, serving the semiconductor and renewable energy industries. Founded in 2023, we focus on bridging two key pillars of the future: Semiconductors and Clean Energy.

We specialize in the supply of high-quality silicon wafers, substrates, and critical materials for semiconductor research and fabrication, supporting both laboratory-scale innovation and industrial manufacturing. In parallel, we provide advanced materials, equipment, and catalytic systems for renewable energy applications, enabling more efficient and sustainable processes.

Beyond materials, Digifund also offers cleanroom and laboratory equipment, as well as high-purity chemicals and consumables for high-tech industries. With a commitment to quality, reliability, and technical excellence, we aim to be a long-term partner supporting our customers’ innovation and growth.

WHY CHOOSE US

Why Choose Digifund

Silicon Wafer & Semiconductor Materials Supplier in Vietnam

Digifund is committed to supplying the highest-quality silicon wafers under strict quality control. We are a supplier of high-purity silicon wafers, substrates and semiconductor materials in Vietnam — the base material for fabricating semiconductor chips, integrated circuits, sensors and solar cells — with a wide range of wafers (Si, SiC, GaAs, Sapphire, InP, SOI) in sizes from 2 inch to 12 inch (50–300mm) for research and manufacturing.

Beyond silicon wafers, Digifund also supplies sputtering targets, high-purity semiconductor chemicals, cleanroom equipment and laboratory consumables — partnering with research institutes, universities and enterprises across Vietnam's growing semiconductor ecosystem.
